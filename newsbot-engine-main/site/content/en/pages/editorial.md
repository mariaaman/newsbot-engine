## Principles

1. Facts come only from a verified fact sheet built from several trusted sources. A fact is called confirmed only when two or more independent tier 1 or tier 2 sources agree; otherwise it is written as reported, claimed or unclear, and disagreements are shown as disputed.
2. We never copy sentences from sources. We quote at most one short passage per source, only when the exact wording matters.
3. We publish few stories on purpose. Publishing nothing is a normal outcome.
4. Stories about deaths, crimes or accusations, health claims, elections and political allegations, religion and communal issues, minors and financial advice are reviewed by a human before publication.
5. Articles are drafted with AI and checked by automatic rules. Each article says so, lists its sources and shows when it was last updated.

## Updates

We update a story in place only when verified new facts appear. Updates are added to the timeline and, if something earlier was wrong, to the corrections log.
